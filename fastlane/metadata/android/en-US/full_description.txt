PDF Viewer reads, fills in and annotates PDF documents on your phone. It runs its own PDF engine, written in C#, on the device. It is open source (MIT licence) and is an early preview (version 0.1).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No permissions at all: no internet access, no storage access.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
• You open a file through the system document picker, which gives the app access to that one file only.
• Edits are saved into the file you opened, when Android lets the app write it, or as a copy that you name in the system's save dialog.
• JavaScript in documents never runs.

READ
• Continuous vertical scrolling, pinch zoom and double-tap zoom up to 8×, with sharp text at deep zoom.
• Outline (bookmarks) and a grid of page thumbnails.
• Search with highlighted matches, and go to a page by number.
• Select text with a long press and copy it. Documents that do not allow copying are respected.
• Night pages (inverted colours) and a dark toolbar that follows the system theme.
• Password-protected (encrypted) PDFs.
• Recent files, reopened at the page where you stopped.
• Open PDFs from other apps ("Open with"), and share the document through the system share sheet.

FILL IN FORMS
• Text fields, check boxes, radio buttons, combo boxes and list boxes.
• Field length limits and simple calculated fields (formats, ranges and sums) work without running the document's JavaScript.

ANNOTATE
• Highlight, underline and strike out selected text.
• Draw with your finger, and draw rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows.
• Add sticky notes and text boxes.
• Choose, edit and delete annotations, with undo and redo.
• A list of all annotations; tap one to go to it.
• Save into the opened file, or save a copy: your edits are added as an incremental update, so the original content and any signatures stay intact. Certified documents only allow the changes that their certification permits.

LIMITS OF THIS PREVIEW
• There is no printing and no stamps, and you cannot move an annotation or change its colour yet.
• Edits that you have not saved are lost if Android closes the app in the background.
• It has been tested on the Android emulator; feedback from real phones is welcome.

Source code, issues and the privacy policy: https://github.com/ramanacr/pdf-viewer
